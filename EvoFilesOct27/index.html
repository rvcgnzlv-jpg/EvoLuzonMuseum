Site Brief

Why This Dig Matters

EvoLuzon is the interactive prototype for a study measuring how experiential digital learning affects Grade 11 students' understanding of human evolution and Homo luzonensis.

🦶

01

Mission

Turn a 2019 scientific discovery into a hands-on learning expedition, not a static article to scroll past.

02

Objectives

Build accurate scientific understanding of hominin evolution while measuring engagement through Kolb's experiential learning cycle.

03

Research Importance

Homo luzonensis was described from Philippine soil by a Filipino-led team — a discovery deserving of a Filipino-built learning experience.

About EvoLuzon

Built on How People Actually Learn

Every page on this site maps to one stage of David Kolb's Experiential Learning Theory (1984) — a cycle of experiencing, reflecting, conceptualizing, and applying that mirrors how real archaeologists work.

Why a museum, not a textbook

Handling a 3D fossil — even virtually — builds different understanding than reading about it. You explore first, then learn the vocabulary.

Who this is for

Abstract Conceptualization

Draw Your Own Callao Cave

Reconstruct what you've learned. Use your knowledge of Callao Cave and Homo luzonensis to create your own reconstruction. Draw the cave environment, fossil evidence, and the features you think are important based on what you explored and learned. Turn what you discovered into your own model of the past.

Your Challenge

Imagine you are reconstructing Callao Cave for another student who has never seen it. Use your drawing to show what the environment may look like and where evidence of Homo luzonensis was found.
